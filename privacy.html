Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

FND Companion is a free self-help app for people living with Functional Neurological Disorder. It was built to respect your privacy: everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

  • Your symptom log (date/time, type, and any optional severity, duration, trigger, what helped and notes).
  • Your saved favourite exercises.
  • The name and phone number of a support person, if you choose to add one.
  • The country or emergency number you choose in About.

This information is saved only in your browser's local storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and no server that receives your data. We do not collect, sell or share personal or health information.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: for example, tapping Share or Download CSV to send your log to your doctor, or tapping Text/Call to contact your support person. That data then goes to the app or person you pick, under their privacy terms.

Links to sources (such as neurosymptoms.org, FND Hope, FND Action and the NHS) open those websites, which have their own privacy policies. The web host that serves the app files may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address) as all websites do; the app itself sends no personal data.

Deleting your data

Delete individual log entries in the app, or clear this site's data / uninstall the app to remove everything. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you.

Not medical advice

FND Companion offers general self-management ideas from reputable sources. It is not medical advice and does not replace your neurologist, physiotherapist or other clinicians. Seek urgent care for new, different or worsening symptoms, or call your local emergency number in an emergency (for example 911 in the US and Canada, 999 in the UK, 000 in Australia, 111 in New Zealand, or 112 in Europe and India). You can set your number in the app under About.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
